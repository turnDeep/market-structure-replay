# v0.12 release checks — 2026-10-19

## Scope and identity

The tested alpha variant is released as **v0.12**, for the two existing markets
`NQ=F` and `NKD=F`. This is a software/model-version release, not proof of
predictive performance or approval for live trading. `NOT_VALIDATED` remains.

The sole legacy-rule override is `buy_reference_radius: 2 -> 1`. The adapter
also supplies strict radius-one high/low pivots once their right-hand bar is
observed, exactly as in the alpha experiment. Existing radius-two and
radius-five pivots remain available. Equal extrema are not confirmed.

`emergency_buffer_atr=0.10` and `major_decline_bars=15` remain unchanged.
Neither beta nor gamma is included. Ordinary continuation BUY remains off;
recovery BUY, SELL logic, fees, slippage and contract-unit settings are retained
from each market's existing saved replay.

## Verification gates

1. Only the alpha override may differ in the executed legacy rules. All other
   campaign, execution and stage settings must match the baseline.
2. The native ledger is rebuilt over the complete history for both markets.
3. Closed trades through 2026-10-14 must match the previously tested alpha
   results in dates, entry/exit prices and native net P&L.
4. Exported arrows, orders, evidence anchors, independent SMA200 and execution
   arithmetic must pass the reviewed view/ledger checks.
5. Both markets pass before production assets/state are replaced. The ordinary
   publisher is append-only within v0.12, with immutable release-prefix hashes.
6. Tests cover strict-pivot confirmation timing, future-tail mutation/prefix
   invariance, seed/source integrity, market switching and display identity.
7. Render both market views in a real browser, including an earlier as-of date,
   decision evidence and selected-date summary. Check download version labels.

The migration receipt, pytest result and browser evidence are completed below
only after their respective checks finish. Cloud deployment and predictive
validation are separate from these software/data checks.
